## §1 MTPFN on the AIR prior (2026-11-03/04, notebook 13)

**What ran.** MTPFN (width 128, 4 [intra, inter] pairs + final intra, 1.84M parameters, AIR's bar bins) trained 100k
steps on the AIR prior (J mix, ECDF g, n_A ∈ 1..10, N_B = 128), seeds 0–2: `results/mtpfn/pilot_20261103-132611`
(3/3 DONE). Diagnostics (`task.mode=air_diag`) on pilot 3's evaluation tasks: `pilot_20261103-235449` / `-235620` /
`-235751` (seeds 0–2, 256 tasks, 1/1 DONE each).

**Deviations.** First diagnostics with n_eval = 128 (`-160920`, `-190701`, `-220253`: the first half of the same tasks)
and a CPU rerun with 256 tasks (`-220644`, `-221440`, `-222230`: different tasks, because `eval_tasks` uses a
device-specific generator). Only the final GPU runs are the pre-registered tasks.

**Result** (J = 2, nats per query, seed mean ± SE): transfer over the clean A-only PFN +1.05 / +0.51 / +0.21 at
n_A = 1 / 3 / 10 (SE ≤ 0.005); gap to G-ref 0.20–0.28; registered-B gain +0.12 / +0.06 / 0.00; conc 0.15–0.20, read
error 0.13–0.17 vs identity 0.07–0.09; PIT KS 0.02–0.04. Rules: "uses B" holds; "registers", "reads globally" and
"warp bottleneck" do not. Paired vs pilot 3 AIR (q-IS K = 32, Σ = 0.1, same seed and task): AIR better by
+0.07–0.09 at J = 2 and +0.03 at J = 16; at Σ = 0 and J = 16 MTPFN is level (−0.01 ± 0.01).

**Interpretation.** A strong baseline: most of AIR's transfer without explicit correspondence; AIR's edge is real but
modest and smaller on wiggly warps. Neither the warp nor registration is MTPFN's bottleneck; reading g from noisy B is.
The pre-registered expectations of a large registered-B gain and a gap growing with J did not hold.

**Next.** No verdict needed (diagnostic notebook). Arm R (paper prior, notebook 12) trainings run as low priority; its
PMC reference is parked (≈23 h CPU for 4096 datasets); proposed instead: 256 datasets stratified by (T, d), ≈1.5 h CPU.
