## §0 Design and refinement (2026-11-03)

**Idea** (Tim, proposal v2): pilot 3's flow proposal reads (A, B) through one pooled 256-number summary c, which
must discover the per-point evidence implicitly, averages over branches (softmax attention) and compresses n_A maps
into one vector. Replace it by explicit per-point correspondence evidence C_a(u), a coherence step through the
warp's smoothness, and a proposal built from the resulting correspondences; multimodal by construction, as sharp
as the data, with an exact density for IS.

**Refinement** (Tim, same day; recorded in the spec's *Refinement* section, it supersedes "MCMC moves" in step 4):
- Gauss–Newton is used to *build* q, never to move drawn samples (that would destroy q's density): seeds (chain
  correspondence sets, or flow draws) → a few GN steps to the mode → a Laplace Gaussian per mode;
  q = (1/M) Σ_m N(θ̂_m, (Λ + F_m)⁻¹). Seeds sharing a mode just weight it more. Flow seeds + GN + Laplace would fix
  the flow's tendency to propose too wide.
- Coherence in 1-D: exact forward–backward along A sorted by x (HMM over B locations; emissions C_a, monotone smooth
  transitions from the transform prior, T(0) = 0, T(1) = 1 as end states); backward sampling gives the seeds.
  In d ≥ 2: a kNN graph over A with beliefs over B locations and learned messages (a GNN doing approximate BP).
- C_a(u) = B's predictive density of y_a at u, widened by σ_A, on a G ≈ 256 grid, once per task. It ignores x_a
  (the prior enters through the chain) and needs h (ranks, candidate h or a learned emission if h ≠ id).

**Implementation** (`experimental/air/chain.py`; differences from the text): LM (damped GN) with a
finite-difference Jacobian; transitions are the exact pairwise prior marginals of (T(x_a), T(x_{a+1})) from 4096
prior draws rather than a stretch kernel; σ_A is latent, so the widening is a mixture over its prior range; a
defensive ε = 0.1 prior share stays in q (as in pilot 3). Proposals compared: `flow` (a), `flow_gn` (flow seeds +
GN + Laplace, refinement §1), `chain_gp` (c), `chain_oracle` (c with the true g; bounds learned emissions (d)).
Criteria V2.0–V2.4 are pre-registered in notebook 14; `flow_gn` was added before the first run (V2.1b, reported).

**Scratch probes** (CPU, 16 tasks, seed-0 checkpoints, J = 2 only; `uv run` scratch, not reportable, no snapshot):
at J = 2 the four proposals were within a few hundredths of a nat of each other at K = 32 for all n_A, with
ESS/K 0.5–0.85 for all of them, and all about 0.13–0.2 nats above the exact grid reference. If this holds on 256
tasks, J = 2 does not separate proposals (the gap to the grid is the shared decoder) and the question is decided
at J = 16. KL(post ‖ q) also grows with n_A for `chain_oracle`, which has the true g, so part of it is plausibly
the fixed fit noise (0.02) being wider than small true σ_A; to check in the pilot by σ_A.
