# AIR-CIS: correspondence importance sampling, unlearned and learned

*Standalone spec and pre-registration. It replaces AIR's flow proposal (encoder E0 + spline flow) with a proposal built from explicit correspondences:*
- *A points claim candidate locations in B;*
- *coherent branch assignments are sampled exactly by forward filtering, backward sampling (FFBS);*
- *a transform is fitted inside each assignment (Gauss–Newton + prior, Laplace covariance);*
- *exact importance weights use the existing decoder.*

*Two versions:*
- ***U (unlearned):** fixed heuristics between rounds;*
- ***L (learned):** a weight-tied operator that sets the next round's proposal parameters.*

*Learning sits only in the proposal, so it can cost efficiency but cannot bias the result.*

---

## 0. Why

Pilot 1 found:
- the flow proposal underfit and too wide (gradient clipping);
- the pooled 256-number summary blurs branches before the flow sees them;
- prior draws and point-estimate slots give no coverage guarantee.

The data's ambiguity is discrete: **which branch of g's level set each A point sits on**. CIS represents exactly that, as categoricals over candidate locations. It samples coherent combinations exactly, and only then fits continuous transforms, inside one combination at a time. Branches are never mixed, and every proposal density is exact.

## 1. Setting and prior (d = 1)

| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Base function g | BNN with **sampled architecture**: depth ∈ {1, 2, 3}, width ∈ {16, 32, 64}, tanh, random input scale $a\sim\mathcal U[2,12]$ (first layer), weights $\mathcal N(0,1)$ scaled by fan-in, output scale $0.5+\mathcal U[0,1]$; then the **ECDF** map $\hat F$, pooled over the *whole* architecture prior (4000 draws × 256 points; recompute) |
| Transform | $T_\theta=\Phi_1^{v_\theta}$, $v_\theta(x)=x(1-x)\sum_{j=1}^{J}\theta_j\cos((j-1)\pi x)$, RK4 with 24 steps, $T^{-1}=\Phi_1^{-v}$ |
| **Complexity, sampled** | $J\in\{0,2,4,8,16\}$ with $p(J)=(0.2,0.3,0.2,0.15,0.15)$; $J=0$ is the identity |
| Amplitude | $\log s_T\sim\mathcal N(\log s_0,0.5^2)$; $\theta_j=\frac{s_T}{j}\tilde\theta_j$, $\tilde\theta\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$; $s_0$ such that $\mathbb E|T(x)-x|\approx0.13$ given $J>0$ |
| h | H0: identity. H1: affine $h(v)=\alpha+\beta v$, $\beta\sim\mathcal U[0.3,1]$, $\alpha\sim\mathcal U[0,1-\beta]$ |
| Noise | convention C2′: $y_b=g(x_b)+\varepsilon$, $y_a=h(g(T(x_a)))+\varepsilon$, $\sigma_A,\sigma_B\sim\mathcal U[0.005,0.03]$ |
| Designs | $N_B=128$ and $n_A\in\{0..10\}$ uniform points; 32 queries |
| Relatedness | Z0 (always related) in this pre-registration |

**Privileged in training:** $\theta$, $J$, $s_T$, $h$, $g$, $T(x_a)$, $T^{-1}(x_b)$.

**Branch label** (for evaluation only): with g dense, $\text{br}(u)$ = the index of the monotone segment of g containing u. A task's true branch combination is $(\text{br}(T(x_a)))_a$.

## 2. Target

$$
p(y^*\mid x^*,A,B)=\sum_J\int p(J,\theta\mid A,B)\;p(y^*\mid x^*,A,B,\theta)\,d\theta,\qquad
p(J,\theta\mid A,B)\propto p(J)\,p(\theta\mid J)\,p(A\mid B,\theta).
$$

h and g are integrated out inside the conditionals. The **decoder D** supplies both conditionals:
- $\hat p(A\mid B,\theta)=\prod_aD(y_a\mid x_a,A_{<a},\tilde B_\theta)$, through the causal mask, averaged over 2 orders;
- the predictive $D(y^*\mid x^*,A,\tilde B_\theta)$,

where $\tilde B_\theta=\{(T_\theta^{-1}(x_b),y_b)\}$.

**D changes from AIR** (retrain under the §1 prior):
- each moved B token gets the extra feature $\log(T_\theta^{-1})'(x_b)$. D then conditions on the full $(B,\theta)$, not only on where B's points landed;
- $n_A$ in training spans {0..10};
- gradient clipping at norm 20, trained until the validation NLL improves by < 0.01 over 5k steps.

## 3. Proposal-side operators (all fixed; none touch the target)

### 3.1 B surrogate (for proposals only)

A per-task GP on B:
- RBF kernel; lengthscale picked from {0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2} by marginal likelihood;
- signal variance = the variance of $y_B$;
- noise variance fitted in $[0.005^2,0.03^2]$.

It provides $\mu_B(u)$, $\sigma_B^2(u)$ and $\mu_B'(u)$ in closed form. Errors here only lower the effective sample size (ESS); they never bias the result.

### 3.2 Precomputed prior tables (once per prior configuration, from $10^5$ prior draws)

- **Location prior** $\pi(u\mid x)$: a 256 × 256 histogram of $T(x)$ given $x$, pooled over J; smoothed, interpolated.
- **Stretch prior** $s_K(\Delta x)$: standard deviation of $\log\frac{T(x+\Delta x)-T(x)}{\Delta x}$, in 32 log-spaced bins of $\Delta x$.

### 3.3 Claims (candidates and emissions)

For each A point a:
- **Candidate set** $\mathcal C_a$ = the top $M=16$ B points by score, ∪ the 4 B points with the highest $\pi(x_b\mid x_a)$ (prior neighbourhood), ∪ (rounds ≥ 2) $L=8$ local synthetic locations (§3.7).
- **Score:** $\log C_a(u)=\log E_a(u)+\log\pi(u\mid x_a)$.
- **Emission:**

| Setting | $E_a(u)$ |
|---|---|
| H0 | $\mathcal N\big(y_a;\ \mu_B(u),\ \sigma_B^2(u)+\bar\sigma_A^2\big)$, with $\bar\sigma_A=0.02$ |
| H1, round 1 | $\sum_{(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathcal G}p(\alpha,\beta)\,\mathcal N\big(y_a;\alpha+\beta\mu_B(u),\beta^2\sigma_B^2(u)+\bar\sigma_A^2\big)$, over a 16 × 16 prior grid (h-marginal per point) |
| H1, rounds ≥ 2 | as H0 with $\mu_B\to\hat\alpha+\hat\beta\mu_B$, using the weighted h estimate, variance inflated by its weighted spread |

### 3.4 FFBS (1-D chain)

- **Order:** sort A by x.
- **States** of point a: its candidates $\mathcal C_a$.
- **Start:** $\alpha_1(i)=C_1(i)$.
- **Transition** from candidate $u_i$ (point a) to $u_j$ (point a+1), with $\Delta x=x_{a+1}-x_a$ and $\Delta u=u_j-u_i$:

$$
K(j\mid i)\propto
\begin{cases}
\frac1{\Delta u}\exp\!\Big(-\frac{\log^2(\Delta u/\Delta x)}{2s_K(\Delta x)^2}\Big) & \Delta u>0\\
\epsilon_0 & \Delta u=0\\
\delta & \Delta u<0
\end{cases}
\qquad \epsilon_0=10^{-2},\ \delta=10^{-6},
$$

normalised over j. The **soft monotonicity** ($\delta>0$) keeps $q(c)>0$ on every path, which bounds the weights.

- **Forward:** $\alpha_a(j)=C_a(j)\sum_i\alpha_{a-1}(i)K(j\mid i)$, in log space.
- **Backward sampling:** $c_n\sim\alpha_n$, then $c_a\sim\alpha_a(i)\,K(c_{a+1}\mid i)$.
- **Exact sample probability:** $\log q(c)=$ the sum of the log normalised draw probabilities.
- **Cost:** $n_A\cdot M^2$, then $n_A\cdot M$ per sample.

### 3.5 Field fit per assignment (Gauss–Newton + prior, Laplace)

Parameters $\psi=\theta$ (H0) or $(\theta,\alpha,\beta)$ (H1), with a Gaussian prior:
- $\theta$: $\mathcal N(0,\Lambda_J^{-1})$, $\Lambda_J=\mathrm{diag}(j^2/\bar s_T^2)$;
- $(\alpha,\beta)$: moment-matched Gaussian of their uniform priors.

**Steps, for each sampled assignment c and each $J\in\{2,4,8,16\}$:**
1. **Initialise** by ridge regression on the linearised map $T_\theta(x)\approx x+v_\theta(x)$, so that $T(x_a)\approx u_{c_a}$. Use location noise $\tau_a$ = the median distance from $u_{c_a}$ to its 2 nearest candidates.
2. **Gauss–Newton with Levenberg–Marquardt damping, 5 iterations,** on
   $$
   \min_\psi\sum_a\frac{e_a(\psi)^2}{2\sigma_a^2}+\tfrac12\psi^\top\Lambda_\psi\psi,\qquad e_a=y_a-h_\psi\big(\mu_B(T_\theta(x_a))\big),\ \ \sigma_a^2=\bar\sigma_A^2+\beta^2\sigma_B^2(T_\theta(x_a)).
   $$
   The update is $(\Lambda_\psi+\sum_a\sigma_a^{-2}r_ar_a^\top+\lambda_{\text{LM}}I)\,\delta=\sum_a\sigma_a^{-2}r_ae_a-\Lambda_\psi\psi$, with $r_a=-\partial e_a/\partial\psi$ by autograd through RK4.
3. **Laplace:** $H=\Lambda_\psi+\sum_a\sigma_a^{-2}r_ar_a^\top$ at the mode $\hat\psi$. The proposal for θ is the **θ-block marginal** $q(\theta\mid c,J)=\mathcal N(\hat\theta,[H^{-1}]_{\theta\theta})$. h is never sampled; the decoder integrates it.
4. **Laplace evidence:** $\log\hat Z_J=\sum_a\log\mathcal N(e_a;0,\sigma_a^2)-\tfrac12\hat\psi^\top\Lambda_\psi\hat\psi-\tfrac12\log\det(I+\Lambda_\psi^{-1}F)$. For $J=0$: no θ, only the $(\alpha,\beta)$ fit for H1.
5. **Complexity proposal:** $q(J\mid c)\propto p(J)\,\hat Z_J$, floored so that each J has at least 0.02.

### 3.6 Sampling and weights

$$
c\sim q(c),\quad J\sim q(J\mid c),\quad \theta\sim q_{\text{mix}}(\theta\mid c,J)=(1-\epsilon)\,q(\theta\mid c,J)+\epsilon\,p(\theta\mid J),\qquad \epsilon=0.1 .
$$

**Auxiliary target** (keeps the marginal over (J, θ) exactly equal to the posterior):

$$
\pi(c,J,\theta)=p(J,\theta\mid A,B)\;r(c\mid\theta),\qquad
r(c\mid\theta)=\prod_a\operatorname{softmax}_{u\in\mathcal C_a}\Big(-\tfrac{(u-T_\theta(x_a))^2}{2\tau_a^2}\Big)_{c_a}.
$$

**Weights:**

$$
\log w=\log p(J)+\log p(\theta\mid J)+\log\hat p(A\mid B,\theta)+\log r(c\mid\theta)-\log q(c)-\log q(J\mid c)-\log q_{\text{mix}}(\theta\mid c,J).
$$

**Output:**
- $\hat p(y^*)=\sum_k\operatorname{softmax}(\log w)_k\,D(y^*\mid x^*,A,\tilde B_{\theta_k})$;
- $\log\hat Z_1=\operatorname{logsumexp}(\log w)-\log K$, kept for the gate later.

### 3.7 Rounds (adaptive importance sampling)

- $R=3$ rounds. Rounds 1 and 2 use $K_r=32$ samples each and **surrogate weights**: $\hat p(A\mid B,\theta)$ replaced by the GP likelihood $\prod_a\mathcal N(y_a;h(\mu_B(T_\theta(x_a))),\sigma_a^2)$. The last round uses $K$ samples with **decoder weights**.
- **The estimate uses the last round only.** Earlier rounds only shape the proposal, so their weights never enter the estimate, and validity is untouched.

**Unlearned update (U) after round r:**
1. **Location prior:** $\pi^{(r+1)}(u\mid x_a)=0.8\cdot\text{KDE}_w\{T_{\theta_k}(x_a)\}+0.2\cdot\pi(u\mid x_a)$. The KDE bandwidth is max(weighted standard deviation, 0.01).
2. **Local candidates:** 8 locations drawn from that KDE, valued through $\mu_B$.
3. **h (H1):** $(\hat\alpha,\hat\beta)$ = the weighted mean of the fitted values; the spread inflates the emission.
4. **Transition width:** unchanged.

### 3.8 Learned update (L): the proposal operator $O_\phi$

**State after round r:**
- per A point: $(x_a,y_a)$, the weighted mean and standard deviation of $T_{\theta_k}(x_a)$, its top-3 weighted modes (KDE peaks with masses), the weighted residual $\bar e_a$;
- per candidate: $(u,\mu_B(u),\sigma_B(u),\text{source flag})$;
- global: the weighted $(\hat\alpha,\hat\beta)$ and their spread, ESS/K, the weighted distribution over J, and the round index.

**Network:**
- a 4-layer transformer, width 128;
- A tokens cross-attend to their own candidate tokens; a global token;
- weights shared across rounds.

**Outputs, for round r + 1:**
1. a claim-logit correction $\Delta\ell_a(u)$ per candidate, through a bilinear head;
2. a per-edge transition-width multiplier $\exp(\kappa_{a,a+1})$;
3. emission h parameters (mean and log-variance of $\alpha,\beta$);
4. a complexity-logit correction $\Delta\ell_J$ added to $\log q(J\mid c)$.

FFBS, fit, Laplace and weights stay **exactly as in U**. Whatever $O_\phi$ outputs, the proposal is a known density, so the weights stay valid.

**Training (privileged truth):**
- $c^{\text{true},(r)}_a$ = the candidate in $\mathcal C^{(r)}_a$ nearest to $T(x_a)$.
- Loss, summed over rounds 2…R:
  $$
  \mathcal L_O=-\log q^{(r)}\big(c^{\text{true},(r)}\big)-\log q^{(r)}\big(J^{\text{true}}\mid c^{\text{true},(r)}\big).
  $$
- $\log q(c^{\text{true}})$ is exact and differentiable: the log of start × emissions × transitions along the path, minus the log forward total.
- Stop gradients across rounds (states come from samples).
- **Curriculum:** the first 30 % of steps use round-1 states from U.
- Training budget: $K_r=16$ per round; 60k steps, batch 32; AdamW $3\cdot10^{-4}$, clip 20.

## 5. Metrics

Per $n_A\in\{1,2,3,5,8,10\}$, total $K\in\{32,128\}$, 256 tasks per cell, 3 seeds for learned parts:

| Metric | Measures |
|---|---|
| ESS/K | proposal efficiency |
| **branch hit rate** | fraction of tasks where at least one final-round sample has the true branch combination |
| q-mass on the true branch combination | how much proposal mass lands on the truth |
| NLL gap to IS-ref | proposal error (the decoder held fixed) |
| NLL gap to G-ref | total error (J = 2 stratum) |
| SBC | ranks of $\theta_{\text{true}}$ and $J_{\text{true}}$ under the weighted samples |
| calibration of p(J) | does the complexity posterior match frequencies |
| runtime per task | wall time, by stage |

Stratify by the number of extrema of g: 1, 2, ≥ 3.

## 6. Hypotheses (pre-registered)

- **C1 (coverage).** U with R = 1, at K = 128:
  - branch hit rate ≥ 0.95 at $n_A\ge2$ on every extrema stratum;
  - AIR-flow lower on the ≥ 3-extrema stratum.
- **C2 (efficiency).** U (R = 1) has ESS/K ≥ 3 × AIR-flow at $n_A\ge2$ (K = 128).
- **C3 (accuracy).** U (R = 3): NLL gap to IS-ref ≤ 0.03 at K = 128 for $n_A\ge2$.
- **C4 (rounds help).** U (R = 3) has ESS ≥ 1.5 × U (R = 1) at $n_A\ge5$, and under H1 at $n_A\ge3$.
- **C5 ($n_A$ = 1 stays hard).** The gap to IS-ref at $n_A=1$ exceeds the gap at $n_A\ge2$. This is expected and documented: it motivates the Σ-smoothing.
- **C6 (h).** Under H1, U (R = 3) keeps a branch hit rate ≥ 0.9 at $n_A\ge3$.
- **C7 (learned).** L (R = 3) has ESS ≥ U (R = 3) at equal K. The gain is largest under H1 at $n_A\ge3$, and the branch hit rate is not lower.
- **C8 (decoder fix).** The $T'$ feature lowers the median evidence KL of V1.2 (reported, no threshold).

**Decision:**
- C1–C3 pass → CIS replaces the flow proposal.
- C7 passes → the learned operator becomes the default for H1 and for the d = 2 stage.
- C1 fails → inspect pruning (candidates lost) before anything else.

## 7. d = 2 outline (separate pre-registration later)

- **Spanning tree.** Replace the chain by a minimum spanning tree over A in A's coordinates, rooted at the point nearest the centre. Run FFBS on the tree (leaves to root, then sample down).
- **Transitions:** a Gaussian on $\Delta u-\Delta x$ with a covariance from the prior's local Jacobian.
- **Candidates:** B points plus round-≥ 2 local locations on each A point's estimated level-set contour.
- **Prior:** separable-dominant T, and g with intrinsic dimension $k<d$. Log $k$, the split and the coefficients per axis (standing decision).

## Addendum before the first run (2026-11-04): scope, deviations, comparison with MTPFN

Written before any code, after the spec above was committed verbatim (57954be). The spec's definitions and C1–C8
are unchanged; what differs is listed here, with the reason. Notebook: `notebooks/17_2026-11-04_air_cis.ipynb`
(standalone description of the idea, the setup and the criteria below). Issue #22, branch `exp/22-air-cis`.

**Scope (stage 1).** S0 (prior, decoder with the T′ feature) and U with R = 1 and R = 3 (S1–S3), d = 1, seed 0
for every trained component. Stage 2 (the learned operator L, S4/S5, C7) runs only after stage 1, and only if C1
passes (the spec's own stop rule, §7 of the instructions). The C8 ablation decoder (no T′ feature) is trained if
GPU time allows; reported if run.

**Deviations.**
- **D1, windowed transform (no boundary leak).** §1's T = Φ₁^v fixes both ends (v ∝ x(1 − x)), so under H0
  A's values at x = 0 and x = 1 equal B's exactly, and J = 0 would make A = B. Found on 2026-11-04 in the
  notebook-16 prior (same flow): with h = id, |f_A − f_B| was 0.003 within 0.02 of an end against 0.055 in the
  middle, and MTPFN's gain over A-only at n_A = 3 was 1.04 nats near the ends against 0.61 in the middle (scratch
  probe, not reportable; recorded in the #20 log). Here T(x) = c + w · Φ₁^v(x), w ~ U[0.5, 1], c ~ U[0, 1 − w]
  (Gaussian latents through Φ, so the whole transform latent is N(0, I)), for every J, J = 0 included (then T is
  a pure affine registration). B in A's frame: T⁻¹(x_b) = Φ̄₁^{−v}((x_b − c)/w), with Φ̄ the flow on [0, 1] and
  the identity outside, so B points outside A's window keep their order and are never masked; the T′ feature is
  log (T⁻¹)′(x_b) = log (Φ̄^{−v})′ − log w.
- **D2, h latent.** One prior with h = id with probability 0.5, else affine H1 (as notebook 16), instead of
  separate H0 and H1 runs (half the training). H0/H1 statements (C4, C6) are read on the true-h strata.
  The proposal does not know h: emissions are the 0.5/0.5 mixture of the H0 and H1 (round-1) emissions, and the
  fit (§3.5) is run for both h types; q(J | c) ∝ p(J) Σ_h p(h) Ẑ_{J,h} and q(θ | c, J) is the mixture of the two
  Laplace Gaussians weighted by p(h) Ẑ_{J,h} (an exact density, so the weights stay exact).
- **D3, transition centre.** The stretch kernel is centred at the prior's mean log-stretch m_K(Δx) (stored with
  s_K in the prior tables): the window makes slopes w · Φ′ with E w = 0.75, so a kernel centred at 1 is biased.
- **D4, references.** G-ref is replaced by the **exact oracle**: the posterior predictive with the true g and σ_A
  (h integrated analytically, as notebook 16), the transform latents by likelihood-tempered SMC per J with the J
  mixture from the SMC evidences; validated against the exact grid where one exists. AIR-flow is not retrained
  (the comparison asked for is MTPFN), so C1's AIR-flow clause and C2 are not evaluated. Added: MTPFN (#20's
  architecture, trained on this prior), and a clean A-only PFN (same architecture, A only), besides the decoder's
  null head.
- **D5, s₀ per J.** s₀ is calibrated per J (as AIR's `s_t_for`), log s_T ~ N(log s₀(J), 0.5²).
- **D6, BNN details the spec leaves open.** Layers after the first: weights N(0, 1)/√fan-in, biases N(0, 1);
  first layer as AIR (input scale a ~ U[2, 12], bias U[−a, a]); width-w layers are the first w of 64 units.
- **D7.** σ̄_A = 0.02 as specified; #21 found a fixed 0.02 too wide for small σ_A (costs KL, not NLL), so ESS is
  also reported by σ_A.

**Comparison with MTPFN (notebook 17's own criterion; C1, C3–C6 are evaluated as written).**
- Metric: NLL per held-out query of A (bar-distribution density, AIR's 400 bins), 256 tasks per n_A ∈ {1, 2, 3, 5,
  8, 10}, identical tasks for every method.
- Ladder: A-only (clean PFN) ≥ … ≥ oracle, with MTPFN, CIS-U (R = 3, K = 128), IS-ref (K = 8192, same decoder) and
  teacher (true θ, same decoder) in between; share of the A-only → oracle gap closed.
- Decomposition: proposal error = CIS − IS-ref; decoder error = IS-ref − oracle; amortisation error of MTPFN =
  MTPFN − oracle.
- **GO** (CIS beats MTPFN): CIS-U (R = 3, K = 128) is lower than MTPFN by ≥ 0.05 nats, with a paired 95 %
  bootstrap CI over tasks excluding 0, at ≥ 2 of n_A ∈ {2, 3, 5}, and not significantly worse at any n_A.
  **NO-GO**: at none of n_A ∈ {2, 3, 5} is CIS significantly better than MTPFN. Otherwise **INCONCLUSIVE**.
- Leak audit (a validity check of the prior, not a hypothesis): the oracle's gain over A-only by the distance of
  x* to the box boundary, h = id stratum; flagged if within 0.05 of the boundary it exceeds 1.3 × the interior gain.
- Also reported: strata by J, by number of extrema of g and by true h; calibration (PIT, 50/90 % coverage) of
  MTPFN, CIS and the oracle; the share of tasks where each is worse than A-only; runtime per task.
