**Interpretation.** The GMM-token mechanism propagates a displacement belief, including joint multimodality, at cost
linear in d, and is near-Bayes for a handful of tokens; with many tokens it is much less sharp than the labelled
oracle, mostly in keeping the hypotheses apart. Part is undertraining (D1), part the oracle's knowledge of the labels
(size unknown). **Next** (not launched): longer training; an unlabelled M ≥ 2 reference; explicit hypothesis slots.
