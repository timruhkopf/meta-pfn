## §1 Design and cost (2026-10-21, 19:00)

## §2 Relatedness calibration (2026-10-21, 18:58)

A's function is B's BNN with every live weight replaced by sqrt(1 − σ²) w + σ rms(w) ε. Spearman
correlation of f_A and f_B on 512 uniform points, 100 functions per cell, noise-free:

| σ | d = 1: median [q10, q25, q75] | d = 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | 0.99 [0.94, 0.98, 1.00] | 0.99 [0.97, 0.99, 1.00] |
| 0.02 | 0.96 [0.82, 0.93, 0.99] | 0.98 [0.89, 0.94, 0.99] |
| 0.05 | 0.87 [0.45, 0.72, 0.93] | 0.90 [0.58, 0.73, 0.96] |
| 0.1 | 0.68 [0.00, 0.37, 0.82] | 0.72 [0.15, 0.43, 0.85] |
| 0.25 | 0.08 | 0.18 |

The deep tanh BNNs (depth 8–16) are chaotic in weight space: by σ = 0.25 the relation is gone. The
grid σ ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.1} spans "same", "closely related" and "moderately related". The spread is wide:
at σ = 0.1 a tenth of the pairs are essentially unrelated. That is a feature, because a learner must
detect it.
