**Result (`-162127`).** V0 and V1.1 pass; V1.2, V1.3, V2, V3, V4 and V5 fail. Full table: notebook 10.
- V1.2 median evidence KL 0.11–0.29 (limit 0.1). V1.3 median spread 0.085 and 0.150 at n_A = 5, 10 (limit 0.05).
- V2 IS-ref − G-ref 0.159 and 0.144 at n_A = 2, 3 (limit 0.12, se ≈ 0.03).
- V3 SBC passes, coverage narrowly misses (56.2 %), median KL(grid ‖ q) 0.29–0.87 (limit 0.2).
- V4 ESS/K ≥ 0.42 passes; q-IS32 − IS-ref 0.355 at n_A = 1 (limit 0.03); total gap 0.450 (limit 0.15).
- V5 J = 16 ESS/K at K = 32 falls to 0.10 at n_A = 10 (limit 0.2); gap growth up to +0.12 at trusted n_A (limit 0.05).
- Transfer is real: at J = 2, n_A = 1, NLL −0.38 (A-only), −1.04 (K = 32), −1.30 (K = 128), −1.48 (G-ref).

**Sanity strata.** Identity: not run (h = id, Z0). Unrelated: not run (V8 out of scope). No oracle MC error applies; IS-exact ESS was below 500 at J = 16, n_A ≥ 5.

**Interpretation (post hoc, `-165535`, `-183335`, `-183338`).**
1. The proposal was underfit: calibrated but 1.7–2× too wide at n_A ≥ 2. 60k steps improved validation NLL by 0.53 (J = 2) and 0.74 (J = 16), and passes
   coverage, but KL still fails.
2. K = 32 is too small for sharp predictives: exact draws with exact predictives lose 0.66 at n_A = 1, with the decoder 0.14. The remaining 0.28 of q-IS at
   n_A = 1 (60k) is not isolated; lower effective K is the untested candidate. At K = 128 (60k), q-IS − IS-ref is ≤ 0.01 for n_A ≥ 2 and 0.068 at n_A = 1.
3. Decoder predictive + finite B: 0.07–0.09 above G-ref at all n_A. Decoder evidence: another 0.01–0.08. Noise-free B removes 21–43 % of the median evidence KL.
4. Order dependence is harmless (ESS unchanged with 8 orders). Grid resolution is not the cause (KLs do not fall on a fine grid).
5. Efficiency: K = 32 flatters it. At K = 2048, J = 16, n_A = 10, ESS/K is 0.054 (60k). Gap to IS-exact at K = 2048 does not grow from J = 2 to 16 (trusted n_A).

**Proposed verdict (not recorded).** NO-GO on the pilot as pre-registered; the idea is not refuted. Next: train q to a real plateau, re-state V4/V5 at K ≈ 128 in a
new pre-registration, and test the n_A = 1 remainder. Notebook 10, report export `reports/figures/air/10_air_report.html`.

## §2 Pilot 2: ECDF-normalised prior (2026-11-03)

**What ran.** Same code, settings and criteria as pilot 1, with g normalised by the global prior ECDF (`task.g_norm=ecdf`; pre-registered in
notebook 10 before the run). Sweeps, all DONE (0 failed / started / missing): decoder `pilot_20261103-090459`, proposals `pilot_20261103-094425`,
evaluation `pilot_20261103-104807`; snapshots in `results/air/`. Proposal sweep `pilot_20261103-090502` trained under min-max because the pipeline did
not pass `g_norm` to proposal training (my bug, fixed with a regression test); it was killed and is invalid.

**Result (`-104807`).** Fails as pre-registered: V0, V1.2, V1.3, V2, V3 (coverage, KL), V4 (two of three). Passes: V1.1, SBC, V4 ESS, both V5 criteria.
- q-IS(32) − IS-ref: 0.047, 0.015, 0.026, 0.010, 0.008, 0.011 (pilot 1: up to 0.355).
- J = 16 ESS/K at K = 32: 0.69 down to 0.23; gap growth J = 2 → 16 within ±0.02 at every n_A (IS-exact ESS ≥ 874).
- IS-ref − G-ref: 0.14–0.20 (pilot 1: 0.09–0.16). Median KL(grid ‖ q): 0.13–0.55. Evidence KL: 0.09–0.34; permutation spread 0.068 at n_A = 10.
- Transfer at J = 2: n_A = 1, −0.79 (A-only) → −1.95 (K = 32); n_A = 5, −1.88 → −2.33.

**Interpretation.** The edge leak is gone (values at x = 0 distributed as at x = 0.5). The posterior over θ is wider under this prior (|T̂ − T| 0.041 vs
0.013 at n_A = 10), so small K suffices and the proposal criteria nearly pass; that reflects an easier inference problem, not a better q. The decoder
and finite-B gap grows, because functions span about a quarter of the scale, so 128 noisy B points pin g down less well. V0 fails because G-ref (g known
exactly) now beats the teacher at large n_A: a reference artefact. Proposed NO-GO as registered; not recorded. Pilot 3 (`air_rb`, notebook 11) uses
this prior.
